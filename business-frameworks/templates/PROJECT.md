# CLIENT BRIEF Template

Template for `.planning/PROJECT.md` — the living client engagement document.

<template>

```markdown
# [Client / Company Name]

## What This Engagement Is

[Current accurate description — 2-3 sentences. What business system are we designing and for whom?
What's the client's business model? What stage are they at? Update whenever scope evolves.]

## Core Value

[The ONE outcome that matters most. If everything else falls short, this must be delivered.
One sentence that drives prioritization when tradeoffs arise.]

Example: "A lead-to-client pipeline that converts cold traffic into paying customers without the founder being the bottleneck."

## Deliverables

### Validated

<!-- Delivered and confirmed valuable by client. -->

(None yet — deliver to validate)

### Active

<!-- Current scope. Building toward these. -->

- [ ] [Deliverable 1 — e.g., Lead intake funnel map]
- [ ] [Deliverable 2 — e.g., Email nurture sequence framework]
- [ ] [Deliverable 3 — e.g., Sales process SOP]

### Out of Scope

<!-- Explicit boundaries. Includes reasoning to prevent scope creep. -->

- [Exclusion 1] — [why]
- [Exclusion 2] — [why]

## Client Context

[Background that informs the engagement:
- Industry and market position
- Current systems and tools they use (CRM, email platform, project management, etc.)
- Team size and who's involved (founder-led? has a marketing person? VA?)
- Revenue model (service-based, product, SaaS, hybrid)
- What they've tried before and what didn't work
- Existing assets (email list, social following, content library, ad accounts)]

## Constraints

- **Budget**: [What] — [Why]
- **Timeline**: [What] — [Why]
- **Team capacity**: [What] — [Why]
- **Tools**: [What they're already locked into] — [Why]
- **Brand**: [Tone, positioning, non-negotiables] — [Why]

Common types: Budget, Timeline, Team capacity, Tools/platforms, Brand guidelines, Compliance/legal, Industry regulations

## Key Decisions

<!-- Decisions that constrain future work. Add throughout engagement lifecycle. -->

| Decision | Rationale | Outcome |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| [Choice] | [Why] | [Good / Revisit / Pending] |

---
*Last updated: [date] after [trigger]*
```

</template>

<guidelines>

**What This Engagement Is:**
- Current accurate description of the work
- 2-3 sentences capturing what we're building and for whom
- Use the client's language and framing
- Update when the engagement evolves beyond this description

**Core Value:**
- The single most important outcome
- Everything else can fall short; this cannot
- Drives prioritization when tradeoffs arise
- Rarely changes; if it does, it's a significant pivot

**Deliverables — Validated:**
- Deliverables that shipped and proved valuable
- Format: `- Delivered: [Deliverable] — [phase]`
- These are locked — changing them requires explicit discussion

**Deliverables — Active:**
- Current scope being built toward
- These are hypotheses until delivered and validated
- Move to Validated when delivered, Out of Scope if cut

**Deliverables — Out of Scope:**
- Explicit boundaries on what we're not delivering
- Always include reasoning (prevents re-adding later)
- Includes: considered and rejected, deferred to future, explicitly excluded

**Client Context:**
- Background that shapes every decision
- Industry, tools, team, revenue model, history
- Existing assets we can leverage
- Update as new context emerges

**Constraints:**
- Hard limits on what's possible
- Budget, timeline, team, tools, brand, compliance
- Include the "why" — constraints without rationale get questioned

**Key Decisions:**
- Significant choices that affect future work
- Add decisions as they're made throughout the engagement
- Track outcome when known

</guidelines>

<evolution>

PROJECT.md evolves throughout the engagement lifecycle.

**After each phase transition:**
1. Deliverables invalidated? Move to Out of Scope with reason
2. Deliverables validated? Move to Validated with phase reference
3. New deliverables emerged? Add to Active
4. Decisions to log? Add to Key Decisions
5. "What This Engagement Is" still accurate? Update if drifted

**After each milestone:**
1. Full review of all sections
2. Core Value check — still the right priority?
3. Audit Out of Scope — reasons still valid?
4. Update Client Context with current state

</evolution>
