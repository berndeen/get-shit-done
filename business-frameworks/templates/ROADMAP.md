# Engagement Roadmap Template

Template for `.planning/ROADMAP.md` — the phased plan for a client engagement.

<template>

```markdown
# Roadmap: [Client / Company Name]

## Overview

[One paragraph describing the journey from discovery to complete business system.
What are we building, in what order, and why this sequence.]

## Phases

- [ ] **Phase 1: Discovery & Lead Intake** — Map how leads enter the ecosystem
- [ ] **Phase 2: Marketing Engine** — Design nurture sequences and segmentation
- [ ] **Phase 3: Sales Process** — Structure the conversion path from lead to client
- [ ] **Phase 4: Delivery Lanes** — Map the fulfillment process end to end
- [ ] **Phase 5: Content Strategy** — Build the content system that feeds the front end
- [ ] **Phase 6: Integration & Handoff** — Connect all pieces and hand off to client

## Phase Details

### Phase 1: Discovery & Lead Intake
**Goal**: Map every way a lead enters the business and where they go next
**Depends on**: Nothing (first phase)
**Deliverables**: [LEAD-01, LEAD-02, LEAD-03, LEAD-04]
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. Every lead source is identified and documented
  2. Qualification criteria are clear — team can sort fit vs. no-fit in under 2 minutes
  3. Lead routing is mapped — every lead has a next step, nothing falls through cracks
  4. Tracking is in place — can answer "how many leads this week and from where"

### Phase 2: Marketing Engine
**Goal**: Design the nurture system that turns cold leads into warm prospects
**Depends on**: Phase 1 (need to know where leads come from and how they're segmented)
**Deliverables**: [MKTG-01, MKTG-02, MKTG-03, MKTG-04]
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. Welcome sequence is mapped with triggers, timing, and content briefs
  2. Segments are defined — different leads get different messaging
  3. Re-engagement path exists for leads that go cold
  4. Clear handoff point from marketing to sales is defined

### Phase 3: Sales Process
**Goal**: Structure the path from "interested lead" to "paying client"
**Depends on**: Phase 2 (need to know when marketing hands off to sales)
**Deliverables**: [SALE-01, SALE-02, SALE-03, SALE-04, SALE-05]
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. Sales conversation has a repeatable framework (not improvised every time)
  2. Common objections have documented responses
  3. Pricing/proposal process is templated
  4. Follow-up cadence is defined — no lead is forgotten
  5. CRM reflects actual sales stages

### Phase 4: Delivery Lanes
**Goal**: Map how the client gets what they paid for, start to finish
**Depends on**: Phase 3 (need to know what's being sold and promised)
**Deliverables**: [DLVR-01, DLVR-02, DLVR-03, DLVR-04, DLVR-05]
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. New client onboarding is systematized (not reinvented each time)
  2. Delivery lanes show who does what, when, and in what order
  3. Milestones/checkpoints exist so client knows where they are
  4. Communication cadence is set — client never wonders "what's happening?"
  5. Handoff points between team members are documented

### Phase 5: Content Strategy
**Goal**: Build the content system that feeds the top of the funnel
**Depends on**: Phase 1-3 (need to know the audience, messaging, and what converts)
**Deliverables**: [CONT-01, CONT-02, CONT-03, CONT-04]
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. Content pillars are defined and tied to business outcomes
  2. Calendar framework exists (not just "post more")
  3. Every piece of content maps to a funnel stage
  4. Repurposing workflow turns one piece into multiple formats

### Phase 6: Integration & Handoff
**Goal**: Connect all the pieces into one coherent system the client can run
**Depends on**: All previous phases
**Deliverables**: Full system documentation, integration map, handoff session
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. All phases connect — lead intake flows into marketing flows into sales flows into delivery
  2. Client (or team) can operate the system without you
  3. Gaps and manual steps are identified and documented
  4. "What to do when X breaks" troubleshooting guide exists
```

<guidelines>

**Phase structure:**
- The 6 phases above are a default starting point — adapt to each client
- Some clients need only 3-4 phases, others need 8+
- Phase count depends on engagement depth and client complexity
- Each phase delivers something the client can review and approve
- No time estimates — focus on outcomes, not hours

**Phase ordering:**
- Lead intake comes first because everything downstream depends on knowing where leads come from
- Marketing before sales because nurture must exist before handoff
- Sales before delivery because you need to know what's promised before mapping fulfillment
- Content can be parallel with phases 3-4 if the client has capacity
- Integration is always last

**Success criteria:**
- 2-5 observable outcomes per phase (from the client's perspective)
- Written so the client can verify: "Is this true? Yes/No"
- Not tasks ("create a spreadsheet") but outcomes ("team can sort fit vs. no-fit in under 2 minutes")

**Adapting for different client types:**

*Service businesses (agencies, consultants):*
- Heavy on delivery lanes (multiple service lines, team coordination)
- Sales process may be simple (referral-based)

*Product businesses (e-commerce, SaaS):*
- Heavy on marketing engine (funnels, automations)
- Delivery may be simple (shipping, onboarding)

*Creator/personal brand:*
- Heavy on content strategy (content is the business)
- Sales may be automated (courses, memberships)

*B2B:*
- Heavy on sales process (longer cycle, bigger deals)
- Content focused on authority/thought leadership

</guidelines>

<status_values>
- `Not started` — Haven't begun
- `In progress` — Currently working
- `In review` — Delivered to client, awaiting feedback
- `Complete` — Client approved (add completion date)
- `Deferred` — Pushed to later (with reason)
- `Blocked` — Waiting on client input
</status_values>
